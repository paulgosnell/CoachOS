**Generated:** January 2025
**Status:** Phase 2 - Search Intent Mapping

## Executive Summary

Research confirms strong search demand across three main clusters:
1. **ADHD + Business** - Underserved, passionate audience (primary opportunity)
2. **Executive Coaching Alternatives** - Price-conscious professionals seeking AI solutions
3. **Decision/Productivity Support** - High-intent problem-solving searches

**Key Insight:** The intersection of ADHD + entrepreneurship + affordable coaching is a gap in the market. Most AI coaching tools are enterprise-focused (Valence, Hone) or generic. CoachOS can own the ADHD founder niche.

## Competitive Gaps Identified

### Gap 1: ADHD + AI Coaching
No competitor specifically combines:
- ADHD-focused coaching
- Framework-based methodology
- Task extraction
- Affordable pricing
- Voice-first interface

### Gap 2: Founder Mental Health Support
Plenty of content about founder struggles, but few solutions that are:
- Available 24/7 (not scheduled weeks out)
- Affordable (not $500/hour)
- Practical (not just "talk to someone")

### Gap 3: Framework-Based AI Coaching
Most AI coaching is generic. CoachOS differentiates with:
- Specific frameworks (GROW, SWOT, etc.)
- Structure for ADHD brains
- Methodology credibility
